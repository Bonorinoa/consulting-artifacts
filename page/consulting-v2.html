How I work

“It is useless to quantify uncertainty without a strategy to reduce it.”

Every engagement ends with what to do next, not with a range. The range is the honest part: when the evidence does not identify an answer, that is the finding — reported as such, never sharpened to look decisive.

Criteria are frozen before results. The bundle is rerunnable by another reviewer. Where a conclusion rests on an assumption, that assumption is named.
